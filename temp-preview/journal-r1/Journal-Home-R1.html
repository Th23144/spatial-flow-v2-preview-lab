Editor's Note 編 者 按

A journal about space does not begin with an empty floor plan. It begins with what we touch each day — a table, a stone, a window, the light that arrives there each morning.

In these pages we look at materials, arrangements and the habits that turn an ordinary room into a place of our own. We prefer patient observation to easy answers, and useful guidance to perfect-looking interiors.

What follows is a small reading map. Choose the question that stays with you.
