01 / Jewelry · Illustrated care

Jewelry

Wear, clean and store with a few simple habits.

01 / Everyday care

Keep moisture and body products away.

Remove wearable pieces before showering, swimming, exercising or sleeping. Avoid long exposure to moisture and body products.

A short visual reminder can sit here later without turning the section into a warning wall.

02 / Gentle cleaning

Start with the lightest method.

For most pieces, a soft dry cloth is enough. If light cleaning is needed, use a slightly damp cloth and dry the piece immediately with a clean towel.

Avoid soaking, scrubbing, alcohol, bleach, abrasive powders, ultrasonic cleaners and strong detergents unless a product page specifically states that the method is safe.

03 / Storage & impact

Store pieces separately.

Keep jewelry in a pouch or box when not in use. Separate pieces before travel to reduce scratches, tangling and pressure marks.

Crystal, stone, metal, cord and plated surfaces may be affected by hard knocks, friction, pressure or sharp contact.

02 / Crystal Objects · Illustrated care

Crystal Objects

Read the material first, then handle and place it accordingly.

01 / Natural materials

Variation is part of the material.

Natural stones and crystal materials may contain small variations in color, pattern, cloudiness, texture or surface markings. These details are part of the material rather than defects.

Each piece may look slightly different from the photos shown online. This is normal for natural materials and individually handled objects.

02 / Gentle cleaning

Dust first. Clean lightly.

For most pieces, a soft dry cloth is enough. If light cleaning is needed, use a slightly damp cloth and dry the piece immediately with a clean towel.

Avoid soaking, scrubbing and strong cleaning products unless a product page specifically says the method is safe.

03 / Placement

Use a stable, dry surface.

For decorative pieces, choose a stable surface away from edges, heat, steam, smoke and busy contact points.

Placement guidance stays secondary to the object itself, but it becomes easier to understand when shown rather than only described.

03 / Home Pieces · Illustrated care

Home Pieces

Care includes the surface, the object and the room around it.

01 / Placement

Start with a stable surface.

Place on dry, stable surfaces and avoid direct heat or strong sunlight. Keep decorative pieces away from edges, steam, smoke and busy contact points.

The image position can later show the actual product in a real placement context.

02 / Room context

Leave enough room around the object.

BedroomUse fewer pieces and keep bedside surfaces uncluttered.

DeskKeep small objects outside the main working area.

EntrywayKeep pieces away from keys, bags, water and unstable corners.

03 / Surface care

Clean gently, then let the object settle back.

For most pieces, a soft dry cloth is enough. If light cleaning is needed, use a slightly damp cloth and dry the piece immediately with a clean towel.

Avoid hard knocks, friction, pressure or sharp contact around fragile or finished surfaces.
