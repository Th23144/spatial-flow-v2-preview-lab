01 · Overview

Keep the original meaning.

This visual system is designed for existing policy and utility content. In production, the original page text, legal wording, dates, links, assignments and SEO content remain the source of truth.

The design layer should improve reading rhythm without changing what the policy says or how the underlying WordPress page is assigned.

↳

The content stays authoritative. This template only provides margins, hierarchy, section rhythm and a calmer reading surface.

02 · Scope

One system for many pages.

The shared shell should support common content without requiring each policy page to be redesigned independently.

  • Privacy and cookie information
  • Terms and conditions
  • Shipping and delivery information
  • Returns and refund policies
  • Accessibility, support and other utility pages
03 · Details

Readable by default.

Long paragraphs remain in Inter at a restrained measure. Editorial serif is reserved for headings, while system labels and dates use small monospace only where it helps orientation.

  1. Preserve the existing URL and page ownership.
  2. Preserve all original policy text and links.
  3. Apply the shared utility-page typography and spacing.
  4. Check tables, lists and callouts on desktop and mobile.
  5. Verify no legal or operational text was dropped.
04 · Reference table

Dense details, still quiet.

Tables should remain functional rather than decorative. On smaller screens, the table scrolls horizontally instead of forcing the whole page to overflow.

05 · Contact

End with a clear route.

Text-heavy policy pages should finish with a simple support path rather than another promotional block.

Questions about a policy, order or support detail should continue through the real Contact or Track Order flow.
