01 · Orders

Before and after checkout.

Order support

How do I know my order went through?
The production store should show the WooCommerce order result and send the configured order email after checkout. This visual prototype does not send email or change order state.
Can I change an order after placing it?
Contact support as soon as possible. Whether a change is still possible depends on the real fulfillment stage and the approved order policy.
Can I check out without creating an account?
Yes in the current commerce path. Spatial Flow is using guest-first checkout, so an account is not required for the normal purchase flow.
02 · Shipping

From studio to threshold.

Delivery help

Where do you ship?
The live page should use the actual shipping regions, methods and costs configured by the store. This preview deliberately avoids inventing countries, rates or delivery promises.
How can I track my parcel?
Use the Track Order page with the required order information, or use the tracking route included in the real order communication when one is available.
What if delivery appears delayed?
Check the available tracking status first. If the parcel has stopped updating or appears inconsistent with the expected route, continue through Contact support with the order number.
03 · Returns

Clear, fair and visible.

Policy-linked answers

Can I return an item?
Eligibility, time limits and condition requirements should come from the approved Returns & Refunds policy. FAQ should summarize the route, not replace the authoritative policy.
What happens if an item arrives damaged or incorrect?
Use Contact support with the order number and enough context to identify the issue. The final workflow should follow the real store policy for damage, fulfillment errors and replacements.
Are customized requests handled differently?
They may be. Any different return or cancellation rule must be stated before purchase and should remain separate from the ordinary returns summary.
04 · Products

Materials, care and placement.

Object guidance

Are the objects meant to guarantee spiritual effects?
No exaggerated claims. Spatial Flow can discuss material qualities, visual order, room atmosphere and subjective experience without promising healing, wealth, destiny changes or guaranteed outcomes.
How should I care for stones, metal and textiles?
Care should be material-specific. The production site should use the actual care instructions attached to each material or product rather than one universal cleaning rule.
What if I am not sure which object to choose?
Use the Services page when you need product guidance, placement notes or a more contextual comparison before choosing.
05 · Services

Guidance with boundaries.

Service questions

What are Spatial Flow services?
The current concept covers light product guidance, placement notes and space review. It is separate from Ink & East membership or future content-system features.
Does a service request create an order or charge?
No. A service inquiry should not silently create an appointment, product, order, payment, subscription or quote.
Is the service a guarantee of an outcome?
No. The service is guidance. It should remain practical and contextual without miracle claims or guaranteed personal, financial, medical or spiritual outcomes.
