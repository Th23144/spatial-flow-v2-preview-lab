Editor's Pick
No. 014 · Hand-thrown · One of one

The Quiet Vessel

Stoneware, slow-fired in Bizen — Hayato Mori, Okayama

Hayato fires this form once a season, in a wood kiln that runs for nine days. The blue-green you see is not a glaze; it is what the ash and the pine do to clay over a long, patient week. We bought every piece he was willing to part with — there are six.

Best on a low oak shelf, on its own, with morning light from the east.
