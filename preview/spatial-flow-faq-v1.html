01 · Orders

Before and after checkout.

How do I know my order went through?

The live site should send an official WooCommerce order email after checkout. This preview only shows the support layout and does not send or modify emails.

Can I change an order after placing it?

Customers should contact support as soon as possible. The final policy can depend on whether the parcel has already entered preparation.

02 · Shipping

From studio to threshold.

Where do you ship?

The live policy should list actual shipping regions and costs. The visual language stays practical and avoids unsupported promises.

How can I track my parcel?

Use the Track Order page or the link in the real order email when tracking is available.

03 · Returns

Clear, fair, and visible.

Can I return an item?

The final return window and conditions should come from the real Returns & Refunds policy page, not from this static preview.

Are custom requests refundable?

Custom or service-related rules should stay separate and clearly explained before payment is collected.

04 · Products

Materials, care and placement.

Are the objects meant to have spiritual effects?

No exaggerated claims. Spatial Flow talks about material, visual order, room atmosphere and emotional clarity without promising healing, wealth, destiny changes or guaranteed outcomes.

How should I care for stones and textiles?

Care notes should be material-specific: dry cloth, gentle storage, avoiding harsh chemicals, and practical handling.

05 · Services

Guidance with boundaries.

What are Spatial Flow services?

Services are light product guidance, placement notes and space review. They are not Project 3 features, membership systems or Ink & East content products.

Do services create automatic orders?

No. A service inquiry should not silently create appointments, products, payments or subscriptions.

Still need a human answer?

Use the Contact page for order questions, product support or practical service inquiries. The real implementation can link this to the existing WordPress contact form when confirmed.
