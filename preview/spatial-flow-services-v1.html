Method

A service should feel clear, not theatrical.

01 · Context

Start with the room.

We ask what the space is used for, where it feels heavy, and what kind of attention it needs.

02 · Object

Then the piece.

Materials, size and color are considered through the room, not as isolated product claims.

03 · Note

Write it plainly.

Recommendations should be simple, practical and usable without mystical pressure.

04 · Follow

Let it settle.

The goal is not instant transformation, but a clearer relationship between object and room.
