## User correction

The user explicitly changed the long-term architecture assumptions:

- Ink & East must NOT be treated as the future parent website for the current Project 2 architecture.
- Ink & East is planned to mature into a separate platform.
- Spatial Flow may eventually participate in that platform as a partner/merchant identity.
- Spatial Flow-authored articles may later be published into Ink & East, cross-linked, or exposed through a merchant-space/article-collection model.
- Therefore Ink & East should not influence current Spatial Flow Home/Shop/Studio information architecture.
- The only likely Project 2 connection is temporary: after the ecommerce reskin is complete, the existing blog subsite may receive a light visual refresh using some Ink & East stylistic cues until the larger platform exists.

## Current architecture focus

Project 2 should now solve Spatial Flow on its own terms:

1. Spatial Flow Home
   - brand/world entry point;
   - not a conventional ecommerce homepage;
   - not an Ink & East teaser;
   - should feel more like an experiential brand index / living front door.

2. Spatial Flow Shop
   - editorial-commerce landing;
   - more than an all-products archive;
   - can carry some responsibilities normally assigned to a commerce homepage;
   - merchandise, collection navigation, product discovery, contextual information and signature-feature entry can coexist.

3. Custom Studio
   - future integrated signature destination/capability;
   - should not require a redundant second full marketing homepage after integration;
   - likely needs a concise immersive entrance plus the actual builder experience.

4. Blog / Journal
   - current Spatial Flow authored-content collection/archive only;
   - may later map to a merchant identity or article collection on the separate Ink & East platform;
   - not an architectural dependency for Project 2 Home.

## Reference-site lesson

A24 pattern:
- main homepage behaves as a brand/activity world rather than a store homepage;
- Shop exists as its own destination and can carry stronger commerce responsibilities.

Squarespace Foundations pattern:
- identity can be presented as an exploratory system/index rather than a conventional conversion landing page.

The useful lesson is not to copy either layout. It is to reduce duplicated homepage responsibilities across Home, Shop and Studio.
