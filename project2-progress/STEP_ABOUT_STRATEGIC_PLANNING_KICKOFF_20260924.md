# About Us Strategic Planning — Kickoff

Date: 2026-09-24
Project: Spatial Flow V2 / Project 2

## Purpose

Begin the strategic planning phase for Spatial Flow About Us.

This phase is intentionally upstream of HTML / visual production.

## Planning questions

The planning must define, in order:

1. What must a serious About visitor understand before leaving the page?
2. What is the factual / product-level definition of Spatial Flow?
3. What is the brand's origin / reason for existing?
4. What differentiates Spatial Flow from a generic crystal, jewelry, wellness, feng shui, or lifestyle store?
5. Which brand ideas deserve experiential / motion expression rather than plain explanation?
6. What should remain quiet and readable?
7. What should the emotional arc be from entry to exit?
8. How should About connect to Shop, Journal, Custom Studio / DIY, and Services?
9. Which parts of the future Global Home / Light Home design system should About share?
10. What content / interaction must still work when motion is reduced or unavailable?

## Current planning principle

About Us is not treated as:
- a legal / utility information page;
- a generic company profile;
- a pure brand manifesto;
- a motion-design showcase.

It is treated as a brand-story experience whose primary job is to improve understanding, memorability, trust, and differentiation while remaining recognizably Spatial Flow.
