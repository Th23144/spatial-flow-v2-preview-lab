# About Us Planning — Narrative Architecture Audit Conclusion V1

Date: 2026-10-01
Project: Spatial Flow V2 / Project 2

## Audit conclusion

The eight-chapter V0 contains the right strategic material but is too segmented for the final consumer experience.

Three corrections are required:

1. "What Gets Lost Online" should NOT remain a standalone chapter.
   - It risks making the page feel like a startup pitch / critique of ecommerce.
   - Its useful tension should be absorbed into the opening experience.

2. "Knowledge, Culture & Ink & East" should NOT currently be a standalone chapter.
   - Spatial Flow must establish its own identity first.
   - The exact public relationship with Ink & East is not frozen.
   - Over-emphasizing Ink & East could make Spatial Flow read as an attached shop, which is specifically not the intended relationship.
   - Ink & East should enter later as context / ecosystem depth, not as a co-equal About subject.

3. "Continue the Relationship" is a closing state / routing layer rather than a full narrative chapter.

## Refined consumer architecture — V1

### ACT I — Before It Becomes a Product
Combines:
- The Object Before the Product
- the useful tension from What Gets Lost Online

Role:
Start from the real object and show what can be lost when materially / culturally rich objects are compressed into normal ecommerce representation.

Rule:
Do not attack ecommerce. Do not delay clarity for too long.

### ACT II — Why Spatial Flow / What It Is Today
Role:
Give the user an explicit, credible understanding of Spatial Flow.

Must distinguish:
- what exists today;
- why the brand approaches objects differently;
- what it is NOT (implicitly, without defensive copy).

This section should establish present identity no later than the first major transition after the opening.

### ACT III — Objects in Relationship
Role:
The conceptual center of the page.

Show:
- object ↔ object;
- object ↔ person;
- object ↔ space;
- object ↔ culture;
- object ↔ time / care.

This is the strongest candidate for the page's second major experiential / interactive scene.

### ACT IV — In Practice, Today
Role:
Turn philosophy into proof.

Use only current or genuinely in-progress realities:
- Shop / physical products;
- real-object imagery;
- crystal / bracelet Custom Studio as present evidence;
- Services;
- Care / ownership support.

Do not smuggle future systems into current proof.

### ACT V — A Wider Field
Combines:
- A Wider Practice
- the necessary amount of Knowledge / Culture / Ink & East context

Role:
Open the future without redefining current capability.

Can establish:
- Spatial Flow is not permanently bounded by crystals;
- future categories may include craft, cultural objects, artisanal luxury, commemorative objects, and other materially / culturally rich physical goods;
- deeper cultural / knowledge relationships can connect to a wider ecosystem such as Ink & East;
- exact public partnership wording remains deferred until formally decided.

### EPILOGUE — Continue
Not a full narrative chapter.

Route the user according to intent:
- explore objects;
- participate / configure;
- understand more;
- seek help / services.

## Emotional arc

**Wonder → Recognition → Clarity → Insight → Proof → Possibility → Invitation**

## Intensity arc

**High → settle → clear → High → quiet → medium → calm**

## Information hierarchy

Every statement in the final About should be internally classified as one of:
- PRESENT FACT
- BRAND BELIEF
- FUTURE DIRECTION

These labels are editorial controls, not necessarily user-visible.

## Anchor hierarchy

Primary anchors:
1. ACT I — Before It Becomes a Product
2. ACT II — Why Spatial Flow / What It Is Today
3. ACT III — Objects in Relationship
4. ACT IV — In Practice, Today

Supporting:
5. ACT V — A Wider Field
6. EPILOGUE — Continue
