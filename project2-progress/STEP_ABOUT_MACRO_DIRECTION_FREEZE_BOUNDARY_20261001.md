## What must be frozen now

The current window only needs to define:
- what About Us fundamentally is for Spatial Flow;
- what the page should make users understand;
- the brand / commerce philosophy behind it;
- the high-level story direction;
- the present-vs-belief-vs-future distinction;
- the level of expressive / motion ambition;
- the requirement that motion remain recognizably Spatial Flow;
- the relationship to Ink & East;
- the fact that current crystal / bracelet DIY is evidence, not the final category boundary;
- the broad chapter / act logic, without committing detailed content.

## Macro direction currently accepted

About Us should:
1. treat Spatial Flow as more than a crystal store and more than a standard ecommerce brand;
2. communicate that culturally / materially rich objects are often flattened by standard ecommerce;
3. present Spatial Flow as a brand that wants to preserve more of the object's materiality, context, relationship, and participation;
4. use current Shop / Studio / Services / Care as present evidence without overstating maturity;
5. make clear that the future scope can extend beyond crystals into craft, cultural objects, artisanal luxury, commemorative objects, and other fitting physical categories;
6. keep Spatial Flow distinct from Ink & East while allowing a deep collaboration / ecosystem relationship;
7. use a stronger narrative and interaction ceiling than ordinary utility pages, but never become a generic motion-design showcase;
8. alternate expressive and quiet moments rather than maintaining constant spectacle;
9. keep current facts, brand beliefs, and future directions separate;
10. end as an invitation to continue exploring the brand, not as a hard-sell conversion page.
