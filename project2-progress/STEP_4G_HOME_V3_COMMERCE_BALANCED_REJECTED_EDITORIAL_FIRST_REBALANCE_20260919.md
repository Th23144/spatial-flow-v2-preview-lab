# Step 4G — Home V3 commerce-balanced prototype rejected / editorial-first rebalance

Date: 2026-09-19
Repository: `Th23144/spatial-flow-v2-preview-lab`

## User feedback

The first standalone Home V3 commerce-balanced prototype was rejected as too commercial.

User statement:
`差点意思，不太行商业化太严重了`

## Diagnosis

The rejected prototype over-weighted commerce through:
- multiple immediate Shop / Studio CTAs in the opening;
- an additional four-link commerce rail directly below Hero;
- Three Ways In functioning primarily as shopping navigation;
- The Edit appearing too early and too prominently;
- Signature Studio being treated as a major sales feature block;
- repeated shopping CTAs across many sections.

The result read as a refined ecommerce homepage rather than a Spatial Flow brand world with commerce naturally embedded.

## Corrected direction

Move to an **editorial-first commerce** balance.

Target relationship:
- brand / spatial point of view first;
- commerce always legible but not constantly asserted;
- Shop should feel like the natural commercial expression of the brand, not the organizing principle of every section.

Revised IA:
1. Opening thesis — brand statement first, one restrained Shop action only;
2. Objects / current edit — concise product discovery, not a sales grid takeover;
3. Spatial note / philosophy — brand thesis and environmental framing;
4. Custom Studio — atelier / making capability, not a promotional campaign block;
5. For the Room — practical spatial discovery;
6. Journal / Ink & East bridge — stronger cultural/editorial bridge;
7. Dispatch;
8. accepted Footer V2.

Remove:
- commerce rail;
- repeated CTA density;
- WEAR / PLACE / MAKE as a large shopping-navigation block;
- overt sales-stat / conversion language;
- large black sales-feature treatment for Studio.

Preserve:
- real product discoverability;
- clear Shop route;
- real featured-product area;
- Custom Studio visibility;
- room-based discovery;
- long-term Ink & East relationship.

## Next action

Produce standalone external Home prototype V2 using the corrected editorial-first balance.
