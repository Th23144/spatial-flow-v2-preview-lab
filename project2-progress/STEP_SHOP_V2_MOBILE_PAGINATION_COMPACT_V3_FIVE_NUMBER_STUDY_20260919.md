## User question

The user asked whether the compact single-row phone pager would look better with five numeric page links instead of three.

## Study direction

Keep the accepted compact single-row structure:
- Previous at left;
- numeric page links centered;
- Next at right;
- no second row;
- no tall pagination block.

Next external study:
- show up to five numeric page links in the center;
- keep current page circle styling;
- retain compact 28px phone number targets and tight gaps;
- preserve enough clearance between the numeric cluster and Previous / Next;
- do not change production source yet.

Rationale:
On the current ~390px phone composition, five 28px numeric items plus compact Previous / Next labels can still fit in one row if gaps stay restrained. Five numbers may feel more complete than three without returning to the original overcrowded 1–8 row.
