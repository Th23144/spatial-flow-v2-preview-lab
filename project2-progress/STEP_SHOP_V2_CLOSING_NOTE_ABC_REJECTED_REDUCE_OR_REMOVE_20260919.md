# Shop V2 — Closing Note concept A/B/C rejected; reduce or remove

Date: 2026-09-19

## User verdict

The three redesign studies (Quiet Center / Editorial Arc / Private Concierge) were all rejected as visually unsatisfactory.

## Corrected design diagnosis

The failure pattern is consistent: every concept still treats the Closing Note as a standalone feature section.

On the current Shop page, the visual hierarchy is already carried by the hero, product archive, pagination and the closed site footer. A large additional closing CTA creates another artificial focal point at the exact place where the page should visually settle.

## New design direction

Do not produce another hero-like Closing Note.

Only two directions remain worth testing:

1. REMOVE — delete the Closing Note from the Shop flow and let Pagination transition directly into the existing closed Footer with controlled spacing.
2. MICRO BAR — retain the backend-editable contact CTA, but compress it into a low-profile pre-footer utility row: short label + one-line supporting copy + understated text/action link. No oversized serif headline, no large button, no large empty panel.

Footer internals remain closed and untouched in either direction.
