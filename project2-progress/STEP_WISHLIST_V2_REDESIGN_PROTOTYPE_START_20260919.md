- preserves real wishlist information hierarchy;
- feels more authored and premium than the old static reference;
- remains quieter than Shop/Product;
- uses selective motion to feel alive;
- avoids decorative motion overload;
- handles both populated and empty states conceptually;
- keeps product actions obvious.
