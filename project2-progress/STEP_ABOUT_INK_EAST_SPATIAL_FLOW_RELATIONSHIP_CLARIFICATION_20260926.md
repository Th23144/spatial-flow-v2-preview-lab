# About Us Planning — Ink & East / Spatial Flow Relationship Clarification

Date: 2026-09-26
Project: Spatial Flow V2 / Project 2

## User clarification

Spatial Flow should not be positioned as a crystal-only business.

Current inventory and current DIY capability happen to be crystal-focused, but future product scope may expand into:
- cultural / ritual / thematic objects and related merchandise;
- genuinely Eastern artisanal luxury goods;
- commemorative / souvenir products;
- other physical categories that fit the wider brand and cultural territory.

The user also clarified the intended relationship with Ink & East:

- Ink & East is envisioned as the larger platform;
- Spatial Flow is NOT simply "the Ink & East store";
- Spatial Flow should remain a distinct brand / commercial entity;
- it may function publicly as a cooperating / partner physical-goods commerce brand aligned with Ink & East's broader cultural and intellectual territory;
- the relationship is collaborative rather than a simple parent-site storefront relationship.

## Strategic implication

Do not define Spatial Flow by its current crystal assortment.

The current crystal category and bracelet DIY are present-stage product expressions, not the final brand boundary.

A more durable working role is:

Spatial Flow = a distinct physical-commerce / product-experience practice that can translate compatible cultural, material, craft, spatial, and knowledge territories into real objects, configurable products, services, and purchase experiences.

Ink & East = the broader platform / knowledge / cultural layer.

The two may reinforce each other without collapsing into one brand or one storefront.

## About implication

Future About copy and information architecture should:
- avoid presenting Spatial Flow as a crystal store;
- avoid presenting Spatial Flow as Ink & East's owned shop unless that relationship is formally chosen later;
- explain its own role and identity first;
- show crystal / DIY as current evidence, not the existential definition;
- leave room for future categories without becoming vague;
- describe the Ink & East connection as a partnership / ecosystem relationship only when the exact public-facing relationship is formally defined.
