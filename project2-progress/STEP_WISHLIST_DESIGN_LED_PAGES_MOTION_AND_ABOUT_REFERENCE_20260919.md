## Guidance for upcoming design-led pages

Motion may be explored selectively through:
- marquee/ticker movement;
- reveal/settle transitions;
- state changes;
- object/typography continuity;
- subtle spatial movement;
- scroll-linked transitions where meaningful.

Do not default to:
- decorative ambient movement;
- motion for spectacle;
- effects that conflict with the established site-wide tone.
