# Footer V2 — Mobile Brand Subtitle Alignment Review

Date: 2026-09-15

## Review target
Mobile main-site Footer brand lockup, specifically the subtitle beneath `SPATIAL FLOW`:

`A JOURNAL OF ROOMS, RITUALS & QUIET OBJECTS · EST. 2026`

## Finding
The overall Footer V2 mobile composition remains visually accepted, but this subtitle should not remain visually centered/indented beneath a left-aligned wordmark.

Current effect creates a split brand axis:
- wordmark begins at the left content edge;
- subtitle begins significantly farther to the right;
- the following `WHY SPATIAL FLOW`, heading, body copy, accordion rows and trust rail all return to the left content axis.

This makes the subtitle read as a separate centered caption rather than part of the same brand lockup.

## Preferred correction
Keep the Footer globally left-aligned and align the subtitle to the same left edge as the `SPATIAL FLOW` wordmark.

Do not center the entire brand block. Do not center the Footer.

Recommended mobile treatment:
- `text-align: left`;
- no auto horizontal centering;
- keep a controlled max-width so the subtitle wraps naturally;
- preserve the current quiet mono typography and spacing.

This is a narrow mobile visual correction only. Desktop Footer and all other approved Footer V2 structure remain unchanged.
