# Step 4G — Home architecture reframe inspired by A24 pattern

Date: 2026-09-19
Repository: `Th23144/spatial-flow-v2-preview-lab`

## User idea

The user raised a structural concern before continuing Home visual work:

- Custom Studio / Bracelet DIY currently exists as a separate site and is near its final visual-design stage;
- in the long term it is intended to be integrated into the current commerce site;
- therefore independently designing a complete Home for both the ecommerce site and the DIY site may create redundant top-level experiences;
- A24 was cited as a useful analogy: the main brand homepage does not read like a conventional ecommerce homepage, while the dedicated Shop landing page is more than a plain all-products grid and carries some homepage/brand information.

## Architecture implication

Suspend further Home mockups until the site hierarchy is resolved.

Preferred future hierarchy to explore:

1. Brand / world gateway Home
   - not a conventional store homepage;
   - communicates the overall Spatial Flow world and routes people into major destinations;
   - commerce is visible, but not the sole organizing principle.

2. Shop as an editorial-commerce destination
   - not merely an archive/all-products grid;
   - can absorb some responsibilities normally assigned to a commerce homepage;
   - combines merchandising, product discovery, selected editorial context and navigation into the catalog.

3. Custom Studio as a signature destination / capability
   - should not require its own separate full marketing homepage once integrated;
   - use a concise Studio landing/introduction layer leading directly into the builder/configurator;
   - preserve its strong identity without creating another redundant “site home.”

4. Journal / Ink & East relationship
   - remains the broader editorial/content world;
   - future architecture should prevent Spatial Flow Home, Shop, Studio and Ink & East from duplicating each other's introductory content.

## Immediate decision

Do not continue Home V3 visual production yet.

Next step:
define the long-term information architecture and responsibility split among:
- global Home;
- Shop;
- Studio;
- Journal / Ink & East.

Only after this split is accepted should Home and Studio final visual design resume.
