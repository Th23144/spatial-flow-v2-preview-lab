Reason:

## Strategic judgment

The underlying user judgment is considered valid, not merely a preference to be mirrored:

- About Us is one of the few pages where a visitor has voluntarily chosen to learn more about the brand rather than complete a narrow utility task.
- Therefore it can justify a higher expressive ceiling than Contact / FAQ / Policy / Track Order.
- A richer narrative and interaction layer can be appropriate if it improves brand understanding, memorability, trust, and differentiation.
- The correct goal is not spectacle for its own sake.
- The page must still clearly answer who Spatial Flow is, why it exists, what it does, and how its point of view differs.
- Advanced motion should support those questions, not obscure them.
- Spatial Flow's visual identity remains a hard boundary.
