# About Us Planning — Narrative Architecture Audit Start

Date: 2026-10-01
Project: Spatial Flow V2 / Project 2

## Purpose

Audit the eight-chapter About Us V0 narrative architecture before moving toward motion planning or final content definition.

The audit will test:
- whether any chapter is redundant;
- whether any chapter is carrying too little or too much responsibility;
- whether sequence matches how a real visitor would understand the brand;
- which chapters are structural anchors versus supporting transitions;
- where present facts, future vision, ecosystem context, and interaction evidence should live;
- whether the page has too many ideas for a consumer-facing About experience.

## Priority chapters under review

Primary anchors:
1. The Object Before the Product
3. Why Spatial Flow Exists
4. Objects in Relationship
5. What We Are Building Today

Supporting chapters:
2. What Gets Lost Online
6. A Wider Practice
7. Knowledge, Culture & Ink & East
8. Continue the Relationship

## Audit rule

Do not preserve all eight chapters merely because they exist in V0.
The architecture may merge, reorder, or demote chapters if that improves:
- clarity;
- pacing;
- credibility;
- emotional progression;
- brand legibility.
