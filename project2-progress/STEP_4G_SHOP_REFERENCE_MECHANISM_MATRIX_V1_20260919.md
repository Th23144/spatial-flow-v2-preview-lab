# Step 4G — Spatial Flow Shop reference mechanism matrix V1

Date: 2026-09-19
Repository: `Th23144/spatial-flow-v2-preview-lab`
Active target: `Shop Landing + Product Archive Hybrid`

## Governing rule

References are mechanism sources, not visual kits.

Do not compose Spatial Flow by combining:
- A24 navigation;
- Palmer drag;
- Squarespace typography;
- Dropbox motion;
- House of Spoils discovery;
- Oura product presentation.

Extract WHY each works, then rewrite it into Spatial Flow's own grammar.

## A24 Shop

### What works
- Shop home is not equivalent to "All Products".
- It mixes current merchandising states such as Recently Added, Best Sellers, membership, category entry and special shelves.
- Deeper shop pages remain recognizably commercial without collapsing into generic archive presentation.
- Product identity and price remain legible even when discovery is editorial.

### Transferable mechanism
- Shop itself may carry homepage-like commercial responsibilities.
- Use multiple meaningful discovery routes rather than one taxonomy strip.
- Allow curated states before the complete archive.
- Preserve strong commerce truth at every depth.

### Do not copy
- entertainment-merch tone;
- dense novelty merchandising;
- franchise/IP dependence;
- A24-specific typographic or menu behavior.

## Palmer Dinnerware

### What works
- Objects themselves carry navigation and browsing.
- Collection exploration is spatial and tactile rather than panel-led.
- Deep collection pages move from collection character/specification into products without changing design language.
- Product count/specification are secondary to object presence.

### Transferable mechanism
- object-as-interface;
- browsing can be driven by object field rather than category chrome;
- selection should alter the visible field, not necessarily open another generic panel;
- commercial controls may remain latent until purchase context is needed.

### Do not copy
- drag-to-explore as a gimmick;
- scattered object field literally;
- dinnerware-specific physical metaphor.

## Squarespace Foundations

### What works
- A small number of indexed states produces a large feeling of depth.
- Each category is a creative point of view rather than a conventional content card.
- Neutral foundation supports content rather than competing with it.
- Tap/explore behavior promises another state without over-explaining it.

### Transferable mechanism
- state-based discovery;
- compact index + strong state reveal;
- controlled surprise;
- one system generating multiple compositions.

### Do not copy
- brand-guideline taxonomy;
- black/white gallery look as a style rule;
- its exact transitions or index layout.

## Everyday^

### What works
- quiet precision;
- large silent fields;
- architectural spacing;
- sparse copy;
- product/system imagery given room to breathe;
- repeated system language without visual clutter.

### Transferable mechanism
- low information density without emptiness;
- scale through distance, not giant UI;
- one primary visual event per section;
- restrained product explanation.

### Do not copy
- home-infrastructure subject matter;
- corporate future-tech tone;
- oversized corporate statement patterns.

## Dropbox Brand

### What works
- visible system consistency across very different media.
- Motion principles explicitly prioritize simplicity, understanding, feedback and subtle playfulness.
- Framework treats clarity as surfacing what matters, not merely reducing.
- Different imagery modes serve distinct jobs while remaining one system.

### Transferable mechanism
- motion only when it clarifies state/relationship;
- shared grammar across different compositions;
- use the right media mode for the job;
- structure > decorative sameness.

### Do not copy
- Dropbox illustration language;
- brand-system layouts;
- playful office/product metaphors.

## House of Spoils

### What works
- discovery exists through Themes, Campaigns, Artists, Search, New Arrivals and Best Sellers.
- product archive remains fully commercial with search/filter/view.
- "explore" paths and archive paths coexist.
- deep category pages preserve the same discovery grammar.

### Transferable mechanism
- lateral discovery;
- archive can remain powerful without being the only entry;
- search/filter are present but not the entire first impression;
- discovery vocabulary can be richer than taxonomy.

### Do not copy
- photography marketplace conventions;
- long theme/artist mega-navigation;
- image-driven luxury travel aesthetic.

## ŌURA

### What works
- product is the unmistakable main character.
- commercial configuration stays readable: finish, price, size, warranty, add to cart.
- deeper product story does not hide purchase state.
- marketing and product configuration coexist.

### Transferable mechanism
- commerce clarity cannot be sacrificed to art direction;
- price/availability/action remain legible;
- product state should be visually coherent.

### Do not copy
- giant product hero;
- health-tech visual language;
- reliance on a singular iconic product.

## Cross-reference synthesis

Spatial Flow should inherit:
- A24: Shop as commercial destination, not archive-only;
- Palmer: object-led discovery;
- Squarespace: state-based controlled surprise;
- Everyday^: quiet spatial precision;
- Dropbox: purposeful motion and shared grammar;
- House of Spoils: multi-path discovery;
- ŌURA: commercial clarity.

The combination is NOT a collage.

Spatial Flow rewrite:
`one quiet object-led discovery field -> one clear archive threshold -> one mature product archive`.
