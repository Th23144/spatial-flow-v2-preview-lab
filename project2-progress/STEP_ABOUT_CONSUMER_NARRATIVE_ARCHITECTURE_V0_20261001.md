# About Us Planning — Consumer Narrative Architecture V0

Date: 2026-10-01
Project: Spatial Flow V2 / Project 2

## Purpose

Translate the validated brand strategy into a first page-level narrative architecture for About Us.

This is NOT final copy, NOT visual design, and NOT HTML.
It defines what each chapter must accomplish before motion / layout decisions.

## Narrative principle

The visitor should not be asked to read a strategy memo.
The story should move from:
**object → loss / tension → Spatial Flow's response → present proof → wider belief / future direction → ecosystem context → invitation**

The page should alternate expressive and quiet sections rather than remain at constant visual intensity.

## V0 chapter architecture

### Chapter 1 — The Object Before the Product
Goal:
Make the visitor feel that a real object contains more than a digital product card can show.

Content territory:
- material;
- texture;
- light;
- detail;
- individuality;
- craft;
- use;
- relationship.

Emotional job:
Curiosity / recognition.

Motion role:
Potential major opening sequence.
Do not explain the company yet.

### Chapter 2 — What Gets Lost Online
Goal:
Introduce the tension without attacking ecommerce.

Core idea:
Standardization is useful for efficient commerce, but some objects lose important context and material specificity when reduced to images, variants, specifications, and price.

Emotional job:
Recognition → intellectual tension.

Motion role:
Moderate; visual compression / flattening metaphor may be appropriate.

### Chapter 3 — Why Spatial Flow Exists
Goal:
Introduce Spatial Flow as a response to that tension.

Must answer:
- what Spatial Flow is today;
- what kind of objects / experiences it works with;
- what principle guides it.

Emotional job:
Understanding.

Motion role:
Low-to-moderate; clarity takes priority.

### Chapter 4 — Objects in Relationship
Goal:
Show the deeper brand idea that objects gain meaning through relationships:
- object ↔ object;
- object ↔ person;
- object ↔ space;
- object ↔ culture;
- object ↔ time / care.

This is the strongest candidate for the second major experiential scene.

Current proof:
bracelet / crystal DIY can appear as one present-day example, not the final definition.

Emotional job:
Insight / participation.

Motion role:
Potential major interactive sequence.

### Chapter 5 — What We Are Building Today
Goal:
Ground the philosophy in current reality and prevent overclaiming.

Current proof can include:
- Shop;
- current physical product assortment;
- real-object imagery;
- Custom Studio / DIY direction;
- Services;
- Care / ownership support.

Hard rule:
Do not present future Object Graph, mature cross-category systems, large maker networks, or large-scale knowledge infrastructure as existing facts.

Emotional job:
Trust / evidence.

Motion role:
Quiet, concrete, product-led.

### Chapter 6 — A Wider Practice
Goal:
Open the horizon beyond today's crystal-led assortment without pretending the future is already complete.

Future territory may include:
- craft;
- cultural objects;
- artisanal luxury;
- commemorative objects;
- other material / culturally rich physical categories.

This chapter can also introduce the principle that Spatial Flow is building toward a more complete way to see, understand, choose, configure, own, use, and care for physical objects online.

Emotional job:
Possibility / ambition.

Motion role:
Controlled expansion, not spectacle.

### Chapter 7 — Knowledge, Culture, and Ink & East
Goal:
Explain only the necessary relationship.

Working public idea:
Spatial Flow is an independent physical-commerce / experience brand that can collaborate with a broader cultural and knowledge platform.

Do NOT publicly define exact partnership language until the real relationship is formally frozen.

Emotional job:
Depth / credibility.

Motion role:
Quiet editorial section.

### Chapter 8 — Continue the Relationship
Goal:
Resolve the story into next actions that match the user's intent.

Possible routes:
- explore objects / Shop;
- participate / Studio;
- understand more / knowledge content;
- get help / Services.

Emotional job:
Invitation rather than conversion pressure.

Motion role:
Calm closing / visual release.

## Overall emotional arc

**Curiosity → Recognition → Understanding → Insight → Trust → Possibility → Depth → Invitation**

## Intensity arc

**High → Medium → Quiet → High → Quiet → Medium → Quiet → Calm**

## Critical restraint

About must not:
- become a manifesto with no factual substance;
- become a corporate profile;
- become an investor deck;
- become a motion-showcase;
- present future ambition as present capability;
- force all users through maximum depth.
