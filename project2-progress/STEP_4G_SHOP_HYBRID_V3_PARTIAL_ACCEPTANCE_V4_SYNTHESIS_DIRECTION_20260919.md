# Step 4G — Shop Hybrid V3: concept partially accepted, overall composition still rejected

Date: 2026-09-19
Repository: `Th23144/spatial-flow-v2-preview-lab`

## User verdict

User feedback on V3 `Archive Is Landing`:

> 创意挺新颖，我还挺喜欢这种风格，但论整体还不如前面的

## Interpretation

This is NOT a full rejection of the idea.

The user explicitly likes:
- the novelty of letting the archive itself become the landing;
- the irregular editorial-commerce composition;
- the fact that the page feels less like a conventional ecommerce homepage.

But the overall page is still weaker than the earlier stronger Spatial Flow commerce benchmark.

## What is worth preserving from V3

1. Commerce from the first screen.
2. Product/archive content used as composition rather than placed below a hero.
3. Uneven product scale and crop.
4. Editorial catalog-field feeling.
5. A gradual move toward normal archive utility.
6. Site-native Spatial Flow palette.

## Why the overall result still underperforms

1. The first screen still has too many simultaneous actors:
   - giant headline;
   - large hero product;
   - two smaller products;
   - browse boxes;
   - count/metadata;
   - grid structure.
   The concept is novel, but focal hierarchy is diluted.

2. The visible grid and panel boundaries are over-explicit.
   They turn active whitespace into a designed layout system rather than quiet spatial pressure.

3. The explanatory copy is still too literal.
   Text explains the concept instead of letting the composition reveal it.

4. The `Current Edit` block becomes another collage section.
   This weakens the continuous-field idea and reintroduces section stacking.

5. The archive regularization happens too late.
   There is still a large designed pre-archive experience before the real utility settles.

6. Overall rhythm is busier than the stronger prior Spatial Flow commerce concept.

## V4 direction

Do NOT discard the Archive-Is-Landing concept.

Combine:
- V3's commercial innovation;
- the prior Spatial Flow commerce concept's stronger spatial control and calmer hierarchy.

V4 should:
- use fewer visible products in the first screen;
- establish one decisive focal event;
- reduce visible grid scaffolding;
- remove explanatory concept copy;
- remove the standalone Current Edit section;
- let irregular product composition directly and gradually regularize into the archive;
- move Object / Space / Material to a secondary edge/index role instead of three equal boxes;
- preserve strong commerce visibility from the first viewport.

Working direction:
`QUIET CATALOG FIELD -> SETTLED ARCHIVE`

Status:
V3 CONCEPT = PARTIALLY ACCEPTED.
V3 OVERALL COMPOSITION = NOT ACCEPTED.
V4 = SYNTHESIS, NOT RESTART.
