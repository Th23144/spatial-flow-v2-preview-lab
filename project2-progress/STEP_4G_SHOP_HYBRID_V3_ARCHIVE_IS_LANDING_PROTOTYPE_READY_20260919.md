## Direction

Mother direction:
`THE ARCHIVE IS THE LANDING`

The prototype removes the earlier:
- standalone hero;
- featured-card section;
- discovery panel;
- separate handoff into archive.

Instead:
- real product identity, prices and objects appear in the first viewport;
- the opening catalog field uses irregular scale, crop, distance and negative space;
- a transitional product field continues the same archive with looser composition;
- the catalog then settles into the accepted regular WooCommerce archive pattern;
- Filter / Sort / product grid / pagination remain explicit in the settled archive;
- Studio remains only a sibling/global route.

## Visual constraints

- Spatial Flow site-native warm paper / near-black / restrained clay / restrained sage palette;
- no generated images;
- static composition first;
- no production source changes;
- no repository static-reference replacement.
